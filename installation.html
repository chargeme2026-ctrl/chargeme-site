Installation

A charger at your home or workplace, done properly

Most installation problems in Ahmedabad come down to two things: the building's existing wiring wasn't sized for a charger, and no one checked the load before installing. We check both before touching a wall.

Process

How it works

01

Site visit

We look at your parking spot, existing electrical panel, and distance to the meter — in person, not over the phone.

02

Load assessment

We check whether your existing connection can take the extra load, and what upgrade (if any) your DISCOM sanction needs.

03

Quote

A fixed quote covering the charger, wiring, protection devices, and labour — no surprise line items after the work starts.

04

Installation & commissioning

Certified electrician does the wiring and mounting; we test the unit end-to-end before we call the job done.
