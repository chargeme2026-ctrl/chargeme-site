Why this exists

Ahmedabad's EV charging network is real, but scattered

Charging points are spread across several independent networks — Tata Power, Torrent Power, Statiq, ChargeZone, Adani, and others — each with its own app and listing. chargeme.in pulls the ones relevant to Ahmedabad into one place, and handles the side most people get stuck on: putting a charger at home.
